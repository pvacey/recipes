# roasted poblano soup
from [suebee homemaker](https://suebeehomemaker.com/roasted-poblano-soup/)

## ingredients
- 1-2 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 medium poblano peppers, halved and seeds removed
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter
- 1 medium white onion, chopped
- 1 cup celery, chopped
- 1 1/2 cups baby gold potatoes, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 2 teaspoons red pepper flakes
- kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 5 cups lower-sodium chicken broth
- 1 1/2-2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into chunks
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1/4 cup cilantro, minced

## process
1. roast the poblanos, preheat the oven to 450 f, lay the halved peppers skin side up on a baking sheet, drizzle with olive oil, and roast for 20 minutes or until slightly charred on top
1. let the peppers cool, then peel off and discard the skins and chop them up
1. heat a stock pot over medium heat, melt the butter and cook the onion, celery, and potatoes until slightly softened, about 9-10 minutes
1. stir in the garlic, cumin, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper and cook another minute
1. add the chicken broth and chopped poblanos and bring to a boil
1. add the chicken chunks, reduce to a simmer, and cook about 10 minutes or until the chicken hits 160 degrees, don't overcook it
1. pull the chicken out with tongs and let it rest 5 minutes, then chop or shred it
1. hit the soup with an immersion blender for a few seconds, leaving some chunks if you want
1. add the chicken, cilantro, and heavy cream back in, stir, and simmer over low heat until piping hot
1. serve with extra cilantro and fresh black pepper, add tortilla strips, avocado, or cheese if you like
