# spicy peanut chicken
from [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DRubuodDlYc/)

## ingredients
- 3 - 4 chicken thighs, diced
- coating
  - 2 tbsp low sodium soy sauce
  - 1 tsp onion powder
  - 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  - 1/2 tsp black pepper
  - 1/2 tsp salt
  - 2 large garlic cloves, grated
  - 1 tsp baking soda
  - 1 tbsp cornstarch
  - green onion whites, sliced
- sauce
  - 2 tbsp butter
  - 2 tbsp low sodium soy sauce
  - 1 tbsp chili crisp
  - 2 tsp rice vinegar
  - 1 tbsp brown sugar
  - 1 tsp sesame oil
  - 2 tbsp peanut butter
  - 2 large garlic clove, grated
- for serving:
  - white rice
  - sesame seeds
  - sliced green onion greens

## process
1. 
1. dice the chicken and toss it with the coating
1. cook the chicken in a hot pan until browned and cooked through, then take it out and set aside
1. mix all of the sauce ingredients together in a bowl
1. melt the butter in the same pan and then add the sauce along with the green onion whites, cook until it thickens
1. add the chicken back in and toss to coat, then remove from heat
1. serve over rice, topped with sesame seeds and the green onion greens
