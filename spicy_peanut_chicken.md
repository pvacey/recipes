# spicy peanut chicken
from [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DRubuodDlYc/)

## ingredients
- 3 - 4 chicken thighs, diced
- 2 tbsp low sodium soy sauce
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 large garlic clove, grated
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- green onion whites, sliced
- for serving: rice, sesame seeds, sliced green onion greens
- sauce
  - 2 tbsp low sodium soy sauce
  - 1 tbsp chili crisp
  - 2 tsp rice vinegar
  - 1 tbsp brown sugar
  - 1 tsp sesame oil
  - 2 tbsp peanut butter
  - 1 large garlic clove, grated
  - 2 tbsp unsalted butter

## process
1. dice the chicken and toss it with the soy sauce, onion powder, red pepper flakes, black pepper, garlic, baking soda, and cornstarch until well coated
1. cook the chicken in a hot pan until browned and cooked through, then take it out and set aside
1. melt the butter in the same pan and add the sauce ingredients along with the green onion whites, simmer briefly until it comes together
1. add the chicken back in and toss to coat, then remove from heat
1. serve over rice, topped with sesame seeds and the green onion greens
