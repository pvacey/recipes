# cereal
from my own kitchen

## ingredients
- cereal of your choice
- milk
- 1 bowl
- 1 spoon

## process
1. pour cereal into the bowl
1. pour milk over the cereal
1. eat immediately

## notes
- milk goes in after the cereal, not before
- don't let it sit too long or it gets soggy
- drink the leftover milk at the bottom of the bowl
