# chicken thighs and shrimp paella
from [Munchkin Time](https://www.munchkintime.com/chicken-thighs-and-shrimp-paella-recipe/)

## ingredients
- 4 chicken thighs
- 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tomato, cubed
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 4 cups hot chicken broth
- 1/4 tsp saffron, crushed
- 1 1/2 cups paella rice (short grain rice), washed
- 1/4 cup frozen peas
- salt and pepper to taste
- parsley, for garnish
- lemon wedges, for serving

## process
1. season the chicken thighs with salt and pepper
1. heat the olive oil in a paella pan over medium high heat and fry the chicken until browned on both sides, about 15 minutes total
1. add the onion and saute until translucent, 3-5 minutes
1. turn the heat to medium low, add the garlic and saute for 1 minute
1. add the tomato and paprika and saute for 2 minutes
1. add the hot chicken broth and saffron and bring to a boil
1. add the rice, turn the heat back to medium high, and boil uncovered for 17 minutes
1. add the shrimp and peas and boil uncovered on medium high for another 3 minutes
1. turn off the heat, cover with foil or a lid, and let it rest for 5 minutes
1. garnish with parsley and serve with lemon wedges
