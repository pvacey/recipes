# deviled eggs
from [Food Network](https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/classic-deviled-eggs-recipe-1911032) (Mary Nolan)

## ingredients
- 6 eggs
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1 teaspoon white vinegar
- 1 teaspoon yellow mustard
- 1/8 teaspoon salt
- freshly ground black pepper
- smoked Spanish paprika, for garnish

## process
1. place the eggs in a single layer in a saucepan and cover with 1 1/2 inches of water
1. heat on high until the water boils, then cover, turn the heat to low, and cook for 1 minute
1. remove from heat, leave covered for 14 minutes, then rinse under cold water for 1 minute
1. crack the shells and peel under cool running water, gently dry with paper towels
1. slice the eggs in half lengthwise, put the yolks in a medium bowl and the whites on a serving platter
1. mash the yolks with a fork, add mayo, vinegar, mustard, salt, and pepper, and mix well
1. dollop heaping teaspoons of the yolk mixture into the whites, sprinkle with paprika, and serve
