# whole30 chili
from a friend

## ingredients
- 1 lb ground meat (turkey or beef)
- 1 red pepper
- 1 green pepper
- 1 onion
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp mustard powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 2 cups beef (or chicken) broth

## process
1. cook the meat in a pot until brown, then remove it but leave the fat in the pot
1. add the onions, garlic, cumin, chili powder, paprika, mustard powder, and salt, and cook for 4-5 minutes
1. add the peppers, tomatoes, and broth
1. return the meat to the pot and bring everything to a boil
1. reduce the heat and let it simmer uncovered for 1 hour
